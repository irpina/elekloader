        About elekloader

        Nothing is uploaded. Your stock OS file and your mods stay in this browser tab; the build runs here, in Python compiled to WebAssembly. No firmware is hosted on this site: you bring Elektron's file. elekloader is an independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Elektron, and flashing custom firmware is at your own risk.
