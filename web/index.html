    Build custom firmware from your own mods

    Bring Elektron's stock OS file for your device and the .elemod files you have. elekloader checks that they combine and builds the custom OS here, in your browser. You flash it like any OS update.

    • Nothing is uploaded: it all happens in this tab
    • Only the main OS changes, so the stock OS always takes it back
    • Free and open source

      About elekloader

      Nothing is uploaded. Your stock OS file and your mods stay in this browser tab; the build runs here, in elekloader's engine. No firmware is hosted on this site: you bring Elektron's file, and your own mods. elekloader is an independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Elektron, and flashing custom firmware is at your own risk.

      Mods are on Modwerk, which builds with this same engine. Mod authors: how to write a mod.
